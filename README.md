# Three-Statement Financial Model — Excel

A fully integrated Excel financial model connecting the **Income Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement** across historical actuals and a five-year forecast.

> **Portfolio disclosure:** This project uses a fictional company and synthetic financial data. It was created solely to demonstrate financial modeling, forecasting, FP&A and financial-analysis skills.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how operating assumptions and business drivers flow through an integrated financial model.

The model follows the core relationship:

**Operating Drivers → Income Statement → Working Capital & CapEx → Cash Flow → Balance Sheet → Financial Ratios & Management Dashboard**

Changing a forecast assumption affects the relevant schedules and flows through the three financial statements.

## Model Period

- **Historical Actuals:** 2023A–2025A
- **Forecast:** 2026E–2030E
- **Scenarios:** Base / Upside / Downside

## Key Model Components

- Assumptions and scenario controls
- Revenue build and growth drivers
- Operating expense and margin model
- Working capital schedules
- CapEx and depreciation schedule
- Debt and interest schedule
- Integrated Income Statement
- Integrated Balance Sheet
- Integrated Cash Flow Statement
- Financial ratios and KPI analysis
- Balance-sheet and cash-link integrity checks
- Executive management dashboard

## Three-Statement Integration

The model is designed so that the financial statements operate as one connected system rather than independent schedules.

Net income flows into retained earnings and cash flow. Working-capital movements affect operating cash flow. Capital expenditure affects fixed assets and investing cash flow, while depreciation affects both earnings and the asset balance. Debt balances drive interest expense and financing cash flows.

## Scenario Analysis

The model includes **Base, Upside and Downside** scenarios to demonstrate the effect of changing business assumptions on revenue, profitability, cash generation and the balance sheet.

## Model Controls

Dedicated integrity checks test whether:

- Assets equal liabilities plus equity
- Cash on the Balance Sheet agrees with the Cash Flow Statement
- Forecast schedules remain internally consistent

The completed model reconciles with zero balance-sheet and cash-link differences.
